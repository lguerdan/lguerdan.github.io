I am a first-year PhD student in the [Human-Computer Interaction Institute (HCII)](https://www.hcii.cmu.edu/) within the [School of Computer Science](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/). I am advised by [Steven Wu](https://zstevenwu.com/), and also work closely with [Haiyi Zhu](https://haiyizhu.com/) and [Ken Holstein](https://kenholstein.myportfolio.com/). 

My work is in human-centered machine learning. In particular, I aim to create systems that augment human abilities (human-AI complementarity), while also fostering partnerships that are transparent, fair, and aligned with stakeholder values. My research is generously supported by an NSF [Graduate Research Fellowship](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/). 

Previously, I did my Master's (MPhil) in Computer Science at Cambridge, where I worked under [Hatice Gunes](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~hg410/). I also studied Computer Science and Psychology at the University of Missouri, where I worked with [Yi Shang](http://dslsrv1.rnet.missouri.edu/~shangy/) and [Tim Trull](https://psychology.missouri.edu/people/trull), and co-founded [TigerAware](https://tigeraware.com/), a mobile-based research platform.
