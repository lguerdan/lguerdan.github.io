Pittsbugh is packed with lovely coffee shops! I have a running list below with notes about my oppinion of the coffee quality (☕), general atmosphere (🌊), work environment (💻 ), and number of wall outlets (🔌 ). 


# Shadyside

#### The Coffee Tree Roasters

The local coffee chain [The Coffee Tree Roasters](https://goo.gl/maps/xv2xmpGJpJZez1gs8) has several locations around Pittsburgh. The one on Walnut is among the largest coffee shops in Shadyside in terms of table space. Facing the coffee shop from the outside, you'll see a large garage door that opens up into a long room with a warehouse feel.

I'm not a huge fan of the coffee here, but the staff is always friendly. Because of the large table space, this is also a great place to come if you'd like to guarantee having a place to sit for a meeting. Lot's of wall outlets too if you need a charge.

- Coffee: ☕ ☕ ☕
- Atmosphere: 🌊 🌊 🌊
- Work environment: 💻 💻 💻 💻 💻 
- Outlets: 🔌 🔌 🔌 🔌
